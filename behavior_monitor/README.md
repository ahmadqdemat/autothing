# Behavior Monitor

A Home Assistant app that learns how your lights, switches, ACs, TVs and covers are
normally used, and sends a phone alert with one-tap actions when something is unusual:

- 🌙 **Unusual hour** – on at an hour when it's almost never on
- ⏱️ **On too long** – on much longer than usual
- 🚪 **On while away** – on while everyone is out, when it's normally off then

Buttons on every alert: **Turn off**, **Ignore**, **This is normal** (it learns from your answer).
