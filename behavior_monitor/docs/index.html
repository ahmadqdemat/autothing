🔍 Behavior Monitor

A Home Assistant app that learns how your home is normally used, and tells you when something is out of the ordinary — like the kitchen lights left on at 2 AM, or the AC running while everyone is out.

How it works Alerts Install Setup Status page Settings FAQ

How it works

You don't write any rules. The app watches your lights, switches, ACs, TVs and covers, and builds a picture of what's normal for each one.

📚 1. Learn

Every night it reads up to 6 weeks of history and learns, for every device, how often it is on at each hour of the day — separately for when someone is home and when everyone is away. It also learns how long each device usually stays on.

👀 2. Watch

Every 5 minutes it compares what is on right now with what it learned.

📱 3. Tell you

When something is unusual, you get one phone notification that says what and why, with buttons to fix it right away.

🎓 4. Learn from you

Tap This is normal and that alert won't come back for that device at that time of day.

Always-on devices are left aloneAnything that is on most of the time (like a fridge) is never flagged.

The three kinds of alert

AlertWhen it is sentExample
🌙 Unusual hourA device has been on for 20+ minutes at an hour when it is on less than 5% of the time.Kitchen lights on at 02:00, normally off after 23:00.
⏱️ On too longA device has been on much longer than it usually is (1.5× its usual longest run, and at least 1 hour).Floodlight on for 18 hours; it's usually off within 12.
🚪 On while awayEveryone has been away for 20+ minutes and the device is normally off when nobody is home.Living room AC still running after everyone left.

What the buttons do

ButtonWhat happens
Turn offTurns the device off (covers are closed). Devices you list under no_action_entities, such as a gate, never get this button.
IgnoreNo more alerts for this device until it is switched off and on again.
This is normalAdds a rule so this alert doesn't come back for this device around this time of day (the hour before and after included). For "on too long", it raises that device's limit instead.

Install

  1. In Home Assistant, open Settings → Apps → App store.
  2. Open the menu (⋮) → Repositories, paste the repository address and click Add.
  3. Find Behavior Monitor in the list and click Install.
  4. Open the Configuration tab and add your phone under phones (see Setup below).
  5. Click Start, open Behavior in the sidebar and press Send test notification. It learns straight away; the first results show on its page after about a minute.

Setup

1. Choose the phones

Alerts go straight to your phones through the Home Assistant app — nothing else to set up. In the app's Configuration tab, list the phones under phones, using each phone's name as shown in the Home Assistant app (Settings → Companion app):

phones:
  - S24 Ultra
  - Abeer's iPhone

Leave phones empty to alert every phone that has the Home Assistant app. Then open the app's page and press Send test notification.

Survives phone changesPhones are looked up by name on every alert, so a phone that re-registers with Home Assistant keeps getting alerts.
AdvancedTo route alerts through your own script instead (for example to add a sound or send to a watch), set notify_script. It receives title, message, tag and actions.

2. People (for "on while away")

The app knows who is home from your Person entities. Make sure each person has their phone linked under Settings → People — ideally both the phone app and the Wi-Fi router tracker. A person whose state is unknown is ignored; leave out entries that aren't real people with exclude_persons.

ImportantIf nobody's phone is linked, the app can't tell home from away and won't send "on while away" alerts.

3. More history with InfluxDB (optional)

Without InfluxDB the app learns from Home Assistant's own history, which is usually only the last 10 days. If you use the InfluxDB integration, fill in influx_url, influx_username and influx_password and it will learn from learn_days (default 42 days) — the more history, the fewer false alerts.

TipThe status page shows which history source is in use and how many days it found.

FAQ

How long before it's useful?

It needs at least 5 days of history for a device before it alerts on it. With InfluxDB that's usually immediate; with Home Assistant's own history, expect about a week.

Does it send anything outside my home?

No. Everything runs on your Home Assistant. Alerts go only to your own phones through the Home Assistant app.

Will it turn things off by itself?

Never. It only turns something off when you tap Turn off on an alert.

It flagged something that's normal for us.

Tap This is normal. If you change your mind, remove the rule on the status page.

Why did "on while away" not fire?

Check that every person's phone is linked under Settings → People, and that the person entities show home / away correctly. Unknown people are ignored.

When does it relearn?

Every night after 03:00, and every time the app starts. You can also click Learn again now on the status page.
